Why Low-Spec Microcontrollers Cannot Talk Directly to Kafka
Protocol Overhead & Memory Footprint
Kafka clients require heavy client libraries, TCP batching buffers, and complex protocol parsers that easily exceed the RAM and Flash limits of low-cost microcontrollers (e.g., STM32, ESP32, or Nordic nRF modules).

Cellular Data Costs
Kafka's wire protocol includes significant metadata framing and heartbeat overhead. In contrast, MQTT has a 2-byte packet header, dramatically reducing cellular data usage across a fleet of thousands of vehicles.

Unstable Cellular Networks
Vehicles constantly drive through dead zones, tunnels, and cell-tower handovers. When thousands of Kafka clients rapidly drop and reconnect, it triggers resource-heavy socket churn and metadata updates on Kafka brokers. MQTT brokers handle persistent client sessions and frequent TCP dropouts with minimal overhead.

Connection Scale
Kafka is designed for hundreds of high-throughput server connections, not 100,000 persistent, idle TCP sockets. Dedicated MQTT brokers (like EMQX or HiveMQ) are built specifically to hold open millions of concurrent, lightweight device connections.
